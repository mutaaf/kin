KIN Support

KIN is a tiny creature that lives on your screen, under your real local sky, and can hop across to a friend's screen. The full how-to is in the player guide.

Getting started

Tap the cocoon twice to hatch your KIN, then name it. Touch the screen to show Feed, Play, Explore and Train. Tap your KIN's name for its card, with Friends, Memories and Settings.

Linking two screens

Open KIN on both devices, with internet on both. Put them side by side, edges touching, and slide one finger quickly off one screen onto the other. That links them, with no codes and nothing to scan. Open Gather and tap Visit to send your KIN across. The iPhone and iPad app and the web version at mutaafaziz.com/kin link to each other. On one computer, two KIN windows find each other by themselves: Gather, then Open a friend window.

Common questions

The screens won't link. Check both have internet, the screens touch, and the finger crosses in one quick slide. In Friends, Who can visit must not be Nobody.

Can my KIN get lost? No. Your device always keeps your KIN's record. If the link drops for about 5 seconds, the visit ends and it is home.

Is there anything to buy? No. There are no purchases, no ads in the app and no account.

Starting over. Release in Settings asks you to tap twice before letting your KIN go. Forget all memories is in Memories.

Contact

KIN is a tiny creature that lives on your screen, under your real sky.

Tap the cocoon twice and your KIN hatches with its own colours, ears, tail and temperament. Give it a name and it is yours. No two come out the same.

A LITTLE LIVING WORLD
• Its sky follows yours: sunrise and sunset, temperature, wind, rain and snow from the weather where you are.
• It decides for itself: wander, nap, bask in the sun, shiver in the cold, or shelter under the mushroom when it rains.
• A garden to look after: a berry plant to water, seeds to plant, finds to dig up and a Garden Book to fill.

HANDS-ON CARE
• Pet it by stroking its head, cheeks, ears, belly or tail.
• Drag berries and snacks to feed it, brush it, give it a bath, and tuck it in at night.
• Flick a ball, wood blocks or a wobbly jelly toy that really bounce and roll.

A BOND THAT GROWS
• Care earns Bond XP, from New friends all the way to Kindred.
• Teach tricks: Sit, Fetch, Spin, High five, Roll over and Dance.
• Sky Meadow, Forest Grove and Crystal Cave open up as your bond grows.
• A personality develops from what it eats, how you play and the tricks it practises, across eight styles from Pop Star to Scholar.

VISIT A FRIEND
Put two phones or tablets side by side and slide one finger from one screen onto the other. That links them: no codes, nothing to scan. Your KIN walks to the edge of your screen and hops across to visit. Your own device always keeps your KIN, so it can never get lost on the other screen, and you choose who can visit.

THOUGHTS
Tap Think to see what your KIN is thinking, or ask it something. A free AI model writes its short, gentle replies by default. You can use your own Claude, OpenAI, xAI or Gemini key instead.

ON YOUR LOCK SCREEN
On iPhone, a Live Activity keeps your KIN on the Lock Screen with Feed, Play and Pet buttons.

GENTLE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-up, no ads, nothing to buy. Needs drift slowly and never reach zero. There is no sickness and no game over, and time away is never punished.
